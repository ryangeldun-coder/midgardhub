Memorial Dungeons

Dungeon gear crafting

Three separate crafting chains, all built from dungeon drops.

Memory Craftsman's Workshop — Prontera (50, 227)

Poring Village's Jellopy Fragments craft into five Zelostones:

Crimson ZelostoneWind of Verdure ZelostoneDusk ZelostoneChaos ZelostoneHoly Zelostone

Zelostone Conversion — Prontera Inn (118, 28)

1 Zelostone (any color) + 1 Zelstar → 1 Zelostone of a different color, your choice.

Accessory Crafting — Morocc Inn (76, 167)

"Suspicious Processor" NPC — each accessory needs 15× its gem + Mithril ×10 (or wait 1 week without Mithril):

Cursed Emerald Earring←15×Cursed Emerald+ 10× Mithril
Shimmering Opal Earrings←15×Shimmering Opal+ 10× Mithril
Ring of Blue Oath←15×Sapphire of the Blue Sea+ 10× Mithril
Red Oath Ring←15×Ruby Soaked in Blood+ 10× Mithril

Mithril is made at "Thor's Flame" (Alberta/Prontera/Morocc/Payon/Geffen/Al De Baran inns) from 10× Mithril Ore → 1× Mithril.

Equipment Upgrade — Prontera Inn (118, 34)

Every Subjugation Team piece upgrades into one or two Expedition counterparts — always 1 base piece + 50× Faintly Glowing Crystal + specific Zelostones. This is what the other four Zelostone colors are for:
