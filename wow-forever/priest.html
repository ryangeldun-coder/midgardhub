Priest in WoW Forever at a glance

Is Priest good for new players in WoW Forever?

Yes. Strong damage while leveling, very high self-survivability, almost no downtime, and it can heal dungeons. Watch →

What is the best early leveling spec for a Priest?

Shadow, with Wand Specialization, Spirit Tap and Improved Shadow Word: Pain. Get a wand at level 5. Watch →

What is the Priest leveling rotation?

Power Word: Shield, Shadow Word: Pain on every mob, then finish with a wand. Mind Blast and Devouring Plague on bigger mobs. Watch →

Can a Priest AoE level in WoW Forever?

Yes, with DoTs instead of AoE spells: Psychic Scream a pack of 4-5, Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague on all of them, kill one for Spirit Tap. Watch →

Is Discipline Priest good for leveling?

Yes. Around 105-110 single-target DPS at level 30-32 with Penance, and it heals dungeons without a respec. Watch →

Can a Shadow Priest heal dungeons?

Yes. Shadow keeps Power Word: Shield, Renew and Heal and only loses Penance. Spirit Tap on last hits keeps mana up. Watch →

Do you need addons to heal in WoW Forever?

No. Raid-style party frames, class colors and cooldown numbers are built into the WoW Forever interface. Watch →

Shadow Priest or Warlock in WoW Forever?

Shadow Priest for versatility, self-healing and fast healer queues. Warlock for raw damage, stronger crowd control and demon pets. Watch →

What are good level 40 Priest talents in WoW Forever?

Ryan runs Discipline 26/0/7 (Divine Aegis, Penance, Inner Focus, Meditation, Wand Specialization) and, on dual spec, Shadow 2/0/31 with Shadowform, Silence and Improved Mind Blast. Watch →

Does Shadowform increase wand damage in WoW Forever?

Yes. The 10% Shadow damage bonus from Shadowform also applies to wands that deal Shadow damage. Watch →

Is Dark Inspiration bugged in WoW Forever?

It looks like it. At 2/5, Mind Blast gives the correct 6% Spirit, but a fully channeled Mind Flay gives 15%, the 5/5 value. Blizzard has not confirmed it yet. Watch →

Can a Priest solo elite quests in WoW Forever?

Yes. A level 35 Priest soloed Mor'Ladim in Duskwood by kiting him around fences, keeping Shadow Word: Pain up, wanding and healing with Renew. Watch →

Where can a Priest farm gold solo at level 30-35?

Dawning Wood Catacombs under Raven Hill Cemetery in Duskwood: level 26-29 zombies that respawn fast and drop Silk Cloth and Wool Cloth. Watch →

How does a healer make mount gold in WoW Forever?

Heal Scarlet Monastery for the last 2-3 levels before 40. Apprentice Riding costs 90 gold at Honored with Stormwind, and the horse itself is free. Watch →

Where can a Priest farm Silk and Mageweave Cloth in WoW Forever?

Sunnyglade, a Twilight camp in Riverglades. The Twilight humanoids drop up to 3 cloth per kill plus raw silver, and the beasts around the zone are good for Skinning. Watch →

Where can a Shadow Priest farm gold at level 45-50 in WoW Forever?

Skulk Rock, a cave in The Hinterlands. With 245 Herbalism you pick up to 3 Ghost Mushrooms at a time (about 30 silver each), and the easy sludges drop Scum Covered Bags and grey items. Ryan made about 100 gold there. Watch →

Can a Priest PvP in WoW Forever?

Yes. A level 26 Shadow Priest won Warsong Gulch with 4 killing blows, about 10,000 damage and 10,000 healing. Watch →

Can a Discipline Priest heal in PvP in WoW Forever?

Yes. In Arathi Basin a level 49 Disc Priest with a PvE build did about 45,000 healing and 10,000 damage, roughly twice the other priest, and the team won. Watch →

Can you buy PvP gear without rank in WoW Forever?

Yes. The level 55 PvP sets cost honor and battleground marks, with no rank required. Priests choose Mooncloth (healing) or Voidcloth (healing and damage). Watch →

How to Heal Dungeons as a Priest in WoW Forever

How do you heal dungeons as a Priest in WoW Forever? Keep the tank alive with Power Word: Shield, cast one Heal only when it's needed, and spend the rest of your time doing damage. Wand, dot enemies with Shadow Word: Pain and land last hits to proc Spirit Tap so you almost never run out of mana. Recorded live in a WoW Forever dungeon at level 30+, November 2026. Most groups in the Looking for Group tool are short on healers, so this is the fastest way to get into dungeons.

Is Dark Inspiration Bugged? Shadow Priest Talent Test in WoW Forever

Is Dark Inspiration bugged in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? It looks like it. With Dark Inspiration at 2/5, the Shadow Priest talent should give 6% Spirit for 10 seconds after Mind Blast or a fully channeled Mind Flay. Ryan Geldun's test shows Mind Blast giving the correct 6%, but a fully channeled Mind Flay giving 15%, the 5/5 value. Recorded in November 2026. Ryan has reported it to Blizzard; Blizzard has not confirmed whether it is intended.
