Shadow Priest or Warlock in WoW Forever?

Ryan Geldun November 15, 2026 18:53 video
Short answer

Shadow Priest or Warlock in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? If you want group content, dungeons and short queues, play a Shadow Priest. If you want the biggest damage, stronger crowd control and demons, play a Warlock. In this video Ryan Geldun compares both at level 34 in WoW Forever, after playing Warlock through WoW Classic (2019) and TBC and testing a Warlock in Forever up to level 15. Recorded in November 2026.

Key takeaways

  • Rotation: a Shadow Priest plays a lot like an Affliction Warlock in WoW Forever. Shadow Word: Pain works like Corruption and Devouring Plague like Curse of Agony.
  • Versatility goes to the Priest. Shadow keeps Renew, Heal, Power Word: Shield and Spirit Tap, so a Shadow Priest can solo hard content and heal itself all day. Warlock utility (summons, Healthstones, Soulstones) mostly helps other people.
  • Crowd control goes to the Warlock: spammable Fear plus Succubus Seduction on humanoids.
  • Executes: every Priest spec gets Shadow Word: Death in WoW Forever; Warlocks get Shadowburn.
  • Gold: both classes farm gold well. Professions and the farming spot matter more than the class. Dotting and skinning coastal turtles is a good early gold farm.
  • Dungeons are a big Priest advantage. The Looking for Group tool is full of DPS, and a Shadow Priest can queue as a healer. A Warlock can't.
  • Questing speed is about the same. Spirit Tap means Priests rarely sit for mana; Warlocks use Life Tap but sometimes have to eat.
  • Far more people play Warlock in WoW Forever, so expect more competition for loot.
  • Demonology pets are a unique extra for Warlock, but managing demons well is a skill of its own.
  • PvP: both are strong. Shadow Priest has a slight edge in battlegrounds (Shadow Word: Death crits, shields for teammates); a good Affliction Warlock puts huge pressure on healers.
  • Raids: too early to tell. WoW Forever changed raid mechanics, removed debuff limits and reworked curses.

FAQ

Is Shadow Priest or Warlock better in WoW Forever?

Neither is better for everyone. Shadow Priest is better for versatility, self-sustain and fast dungeon queues as a healer. Warlock is better for maximum damage, stronger crowd control and a demon companion.

Can a Shadow Priest heal dungeons in WoW Forever?

Yes. A Shadow Priest can heal dungeons while leveling. It takes a bit more mana management around Spirit Tap.

Which class levels faster in WoW Forever, Shadow Priest or Warlock?

About the same. Priests rarely stop for mana thanks to Spirit Tap; Warlocks use Life Tap but sometimes eat.

Which class makes more gold in WoW Forever?

Both farm well. Choose professions and farming spots that play to your class's strengths.
