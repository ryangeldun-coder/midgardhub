Is Dark Inspiration Bugged? Shadow Priest Talent Test in WoW Forever

Ryan Geldun November 22, 2026 2:15 video
Short answer

Is Dark Inspiration bugged in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? It looks like it. With Dark Inspiration at 2/5, the Shadow Priest talent should give 6% Spirit for 10 seconds after Mind Blast or a fully channeled Mind Flay. Ryan Geldun's test shows Mind Blast giving the correct 6%, but a fully channeled Mind Flay giving 15%, the 5/5 value. Recorded in November 2026. Ryan has reported it to Blizzard; Blizzard has not confirmed whether it is intended.

The Test

  • Ryan opens the character sheet with 180 Spirit and Dark Inspiration at 2/5.
  • He casts Mind Blast: Spirit goes to 191, which matches the 6% on the 2/5 tooltip.
  • He fully channels Mind Flay: Spirit goes to 207, a 15% increase, which is what 5/5 Dark Inspiration should give.
  • 15% equals 5 ranks of 3%, so the fully channeled Mind Flay seems to use the max-rank value no matter how many points you have.

What It Means for Shadow Priests

  • If this is a bug, 2 points in Dark Inspiration currently give the full 5/5 Spirit bonus whenever you finish a Mind Flay channel.
  • Ryan is not putting more points into Dark Inspiration until Blizzard fixes it or confirms it, because extra points would be wasted.
  • If you play Shadow Priest in WoW Forever, check your own Spirit on the character sheet before and after a full Mind Flay.

FAQ

Is Dark Inspiration bugged in WoW Forever?

It looks like it. At 2/5, Mind Blast gives the correct 6% Spirit, but a fully channeled Mind Flay gives 15%, the 5/5 value. Blizzard has not confirmed it yet.

What does Dark Inspiration do in WoW Forever?

Dark Inspiration is a Shadow Priest talent: Mind Blast and fully channeled Mind Flay increase your and your party's Spirit by 3% per point (up to 15% at 5/5) for 10 seconds.

Should you put 5 points into Dark Inspiration?

Not right now, according to Ryan. With the current behavior, 2 points already give 15% Spirit after a full Mind Flay, so extra points may be wasted until it is fixed.
