Riverglades Gold and Cloth Farm as a Shadow Priest in WoW Forever (Silk and Mageweave)

Ryan Geldun November 22, 2026 15:40 video
Short answer

Riverglades is one of the best gold and cloth farms for a Priest in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever). Ryan Geldun farms Sunnyglade, a Twilight camp in Riverglades, on a level 45 Shadow Priest: the Twilight humanoids drop Silk Cloth, Mageweave Cloth, raw silver and green items, and almost everything else in the zone is a beast you can skin. He also shows the same farm as a Discipline Priest. Recorded in November 2026.

Why Riverglades Is Good for Gold in WoW Forever

  • Riverglades has quest chain after quest chain, and the quest rewards vendor for a lot of gold. Ryan says the zone feels more like retail WoW in quest density.
  • Almost every enemy in Riverglades is a humanoid (coin and cloth) or a beast (Skinning).
  • Riverglades is densely packed: gnolls near the first quests, murlocs and pirates along the coast, beasts everywhere, and orcs in the Twilight camps. There are 3-4 enemies around you anywhere you walk.
  • An underground quest area entrance sits by the big tree near Sunnyglade. Its quests are easy and give a lot of experience.
  • Higher-level pirates (level 40+) in Riverglades drop the same cloth, so Ryan does not think they are worth the extra risk.
  • Riverglades also has its own dungeon, where Ryan got his dagger.
  • Other places on the Riverglades map: Rog'mar, Twilight's Shroud, Powderfuse Port, Krol'dok Stronghold, Farhold Keep, Windstead.

How Ryan Farms It As a Shadow Priest

  • Pull 3-5 Twilight humanoids, Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague on all of them, Psychic Scream, then Mind Blast, Mind Flay and Shadow Word: Death.
  • Silence the Twilight Shadowmancers. They cast Shadow Bolt and stay at range.
  • Devouring Contagion spreads Devouring Plague to nearby enemies when the target dies.
  • Vampiric Embrace heals Ryan off the whole pull.
  • Power Word: Shield is still very strong on a Shadow Priest: one shield absorbed a full pack.
  • Ryan put 1 point into Dark Inspiration and may take it to 5/5 before level 50.

Shadow vs Discipline for Farming

  • Shadow Priest feels like at least double the power of Discipline, according to Ryan.
  • Discipline Priest can farm Sunnyglade too, but it is much slower, and Ryan misses Silence.

FAQ

Where can you farm Silk Cloth and Mageweave Cloth in WoW Forever?

Sunnyglade in Riverglades. The Twilight Raiders, Neophytes and Shadowmancers there drop Silk Cloth and Mageweave Cloth, up to 3 per kill, plus raw silver and green items.

Is Riverglades good for gold in WoW Forever?

Yes. Riverglades has long quest chains with valuable rewards, humanoids that drop coin and cloth, and beasts for Skinning. Ryan made about 100 gold between levels 40 and 45.

What level should you be for Riverglades in WoW Forever?

Ryan farmed Sunnyglade comfortably at level 41 as Discipline and at level 45 as Shadow. The pirates in Riverglades are level 40+.

Is Shadow or Discipline better for farming as a Priest in WoW Forever?

Shadow. Ryan says it feels like double the power. Discipline works but is much slower.
