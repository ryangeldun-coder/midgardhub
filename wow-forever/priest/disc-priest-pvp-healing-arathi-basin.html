Disc Priest Pvp Tips From This Match

  • Buff the team before the gates open: Stamina, Spirit and Shadow Protection. Shadow resistance can make a Shadow Priest or Warlock spell miss, which matters in PvP.
  • Shield everyone just before the first clash, then shield yourself.
  • Shadow Word: Death finishes low targets and triggers Spirit Tap for mana. Watch for a Paladin's bubble, or the cast is wasted.
  • Shadow Word: Pain still hits hard in this bracket: about 781 damage over 24 sec from Disc (about 1,000 from Shadow), close to a third of his health.
  • Bring mana potions. After you resurrect and rebuff you are at half mana. Ryan buys Superior Mana Potions on the auction house.
  • Dispel sheep (Polymorph) on your team when you can, but in heavy fights healing comes first.
  • Stay on the flags with your team. A healer left alone on a base is the first target.
  • Use terrain to line of sight casters. Ryan admits he does it less than he should.

Healers in Battlegrounds

In his experience most Paladins in random battlegrounds rarely heal, so the healing falls on the priests. Druids are a big helpthey leave their form, cast Rejuvenation or Regrowth and shift back. With more healers on the team, a Disc Priest can cast more Shadow Word: Pain and pressure the enemy.

FAQ

Can a Discipline Priest heal in PvP in WoW Forever?

Yes. In this Arathi Basin match Ryan did about 45,000 healing at level 49 with his PvE Disc build and his team won.

What talents should a Disc Priest take for PvP in WoW Forever?

Ryan would add Blackout and Improved Psychic Scream. His dungeon build without them still works.

Does Shadow Word: Death trigger Spirit Tap?

Yes, according to Ryan. He uses it to finish low targets and get the Spirit Tap mana back.

Do Paladins heal in WoW Forever battlegrounds?

In Ryan's games, most Paladins rarely heal, so priests do most of the healing. Druids help more.
