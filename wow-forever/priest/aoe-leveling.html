How to AoE Level a Priest in WoW Forever

Ryan Geldun November 12, 2026 7:07 video
Short answer

Can a Priest AoE level in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? Yes, just not with AoE spells. In this video Ryan Geldun farms 4-5 mobs at a time as a level 26 Shadow Priest in the Wetlands: DoT everything, Psychic Scream the pack, kill one mob fast for Spirit Tap and let the rest die to DoTs and wand. Recorded in November 2026.

Key takeaways

  • Priests AoE level in WoW Forever with single-target DoTs, not AoE spells. Mage AoE was nerfed in Forever, but Priest DoTs were not touched.
  • The Priest AoE rotation: pull a pack into open space, Psychic Scream, then Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague on everything.
  • Kill one mob as fast as possible so Spirit Tap refills your mana, then reshield, redot and wand down the rest.
  • Always fight in open space. Psychic Scream in a crowded area sends mobs running into more mobs.
  • Don't overpull: Spirit Tap is what keeps the loop going.
  • A strong wand matters a lot for AoE leveling. Ryan uses the wand from the Deadmines.
  • Over half an hour, DoT-based AoE farming is much faster than questing, as long as nobody else is farming your spot.

FAQ

Can a Priest AoE farm in WoW Forever?

Yes. A Shadow Priest can farm 4-5 mobs at once by fearing the pack, putting Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague on all of them and using Spirit Tap to refill mana.

Where can a Priest AoE level in WoW Forever?

Ryan uses the gnolls in the south Wetlands around level 26. They give good XP and drop cloth.

Is Priest AoE leveling faster than questing?

Over half an hour, yes, as long as nobody else is farming the same spot.

What talents do you need to AoE level as a Priest?

Blackout, Spirit Tap and Improved Shadow Word: Pain. Shadow Focus helps because a resisted DoT hurts.
