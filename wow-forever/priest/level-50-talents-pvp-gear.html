Shadow Priest Talents at Level 49 (4/0/36)

  • Dark Inspiration 5/5: it works again. Mind Blast and fully channeled Mind Flay raise your and your party's Spirit by 15% for 10 sec.
  • Same base as the level 40 build: Shadowform, Shadow Weaving, Silence, Darkness, Improved Mind Blast, Vampiric Embrace.
  • For PvP Ryan misses Blackout and Improved Psychic Scream.
  • He still wands a lot as Shadow, because it saves mana.

Discipline Priest Talents at Level 49 (31/2/7)

  • Renewed Hope 2/5 and Divine Aegis 3/3. Divine Aegis: critical heals create a shield for 15% of the amount healed (up to 980 damage, 12 sec).
  • Power Infusion: strong with a caster in the group, a dead talent without one. Ryan is testing it.
  • 2 points in Holy; his next points go deeper into Holy.

Gear at Level 49

  • Peacekeeper Wand (Zul'Farrak): 60-113 Shadow damage, 3 mana per 5 sec, +4 spell damage. Shadow damage, so Shadowform's 10% bonus applies.
  • Shadowlight Athame (new Riverglades dungeon): main-hand dagger, +52 spell damage and healing.
  • Orb of the Forgotten Seer (Scarlet Monastery): off-hand, +14 spell damage and healing.
  • Stick No Rock: staff with up to +101 healing, +33 damage and +18 Spirit. Ryan uses it only when healing.
  • He keeps as much mana per 5 sec gear as he can.

Pvp Gear Without Rank in WoW Forever

  • The level 55 sets cost honor plus battleground marks, with no rank required.
  • Priest sets: Lieutenant Commander's Investiture (Premier Mooncloth, healing) and Lieutenant Commander's Raiment (Premier Voidcloth, equal healing and damage). They look the same.
  • Investiture bonuses: (2) up to +44 healing, (4) Psychic Scream +1 sec, (6) +20 Stamina.
  • Ryan buys Mooncloth: robes (9,350 honor + 15 marks), shoulders (6,350 + 10) and helm (9,000 + 15) first.
  • Marks he needs (his rough count): about 35 Arathi Basin, 15 Alterac Valley, 10 from the new battleground and 25-35 Warsong Gulch.

FAQ

Can you buy PvP gear without rank in WoW Forever?

Yes. The level 55 PvP sets cost honor and battleground marks, and do not need a PvP rank. Ryan buys them at rank 3.

Which PvP set should a Priest get in WoW Forever?

Mooncloth (Lieutenant Commander's Investiture) for healing, Voidcloth (Lieutenant Commander's Raiment) for a mix of healing and damage.

Is Dark Inspiration fixed in WoW Forever?

Yes, according to Ryan at level 49. He took it 5/5 in his Shadow build.

Is Power Infusion worth it for Discipline Priest?

In dungeons with a caster, yes. Without a caster it does nothing.

With Dark Inspiration at 2/5, the Shadow Priest talent should give 6% Spirit for 10 seconds after Mind Blast or a fully channeled Mind Flay. Ryan Geldun's test shows Mind Blast giving the correct 6%, but a fully channeled Mind Flay giving 15%, the 5/5 value. Recorded in November 2026.
