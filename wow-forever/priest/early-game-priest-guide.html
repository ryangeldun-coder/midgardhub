Early Game Priest Guide for WoW Forever (Levels 1-20)

Short answer

How do you level a Priest early in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? Go Shadow, get a wand at level 5, stack Spirit, Intellect and Stamina with +healing and +spell damage gear, and use Shadow Word: Pain plus wand as your rotation. In this video Ryan Geldun shows his own Priest from level 1 to 20 in WoW Forever: stats, talents, wands, consumables, Tailoring and dungeon gear, recorded in November 2026.

Quick facts

GameWorld of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)

Stat prioritySpirit → Intellect → Stamina, plus +healing and +spell damage
Core talentsWand Specialization, Spirit Tap, Improved Shadow Word: Pain
RotationShadow Word: Pain, then wand (Mind Blast optional, Devouring Plague on bigger mobs)
First wandAuction House at level 5 (about 2 silver at recording)
Wand upgradesCookie's Stirring Rod (Deadmines), Lesser / Greater Magic Wand (Enchanting)
ProfessionTailoring (Pristine Circlet from the new Redridge vendor patterns)
Dungeon gearStaff of Westfall and Corsair's Overshirt (Deadmines)

Key takeaways

  • The WoW Forever Priest plays almost like the Classic Priest but feels a lot more powerful while leveling.
  • Stats: Spirit first, then Intellect and Stamina, with as much +healing and +spell damage gear as you can find. The same gear works for questing, farming and dungeons.
  • Early Shadow talents: a couple of points in Wand Specialization, then Spirit Tap and Improved Shadow Word: Pain. Shadow Focus can wait while Shadow Word: Pain is the only shadow spell you spam.
  • Cheap consumables are worth using on a Priest: Troll's Blood, Elixir of Minor Fortitude and Minor Arcane Elixir cost a few copper each.
  • To farm a whole camp as a Priest: DoT everything, Power Word: Shield, Renew, and Psychic Scream the extras. DoTs don't break the fear, so you can pull an entire gnoll camp and walk away.
  • Tailoring gives spell damage, Stamina and Intellect gear from level 10. The Pristine Circlet (10 Stamina plus healing and spell damage on a head slot) is the big target, but its essences, dyes and leather make it expensive.
  • The Staff of Westfall (from VanCleef) and the Corsair's Overshirt from the Deadmines, stacked with crafted gear, let a level 20 Priest heal dungeons with zero healing talents.
  • Early Shadow is much stronger than Discipline or Holy for leveling: wanding costs no mana, spamming Smite does.
  • The 10-19 Warsong Gulch bracket is great on a Priest: Shadow Word: Pain on everyone, shield your team, wand people down.
  • Talent plan after level 20: Mind Flay, one forced point in Shadow Reach, then Shadow Word: Death, an execute for Priests.
  • At level 20 the Priest felt much stronger than Ryan's Rogue did at 15, although wands won't carry you all the way to 60.

FAQ

What stats should a Priest use while leveling in WoW Forever?

Spirit first, then Intellect and Stamina, plus +healing and +spell damage gear, so one set covers questing, farming and dungeon healing.

What is the best early leveling spec for Priest in WoW Forever?

Shadow. Wand Specialization, Spirit Tap and Improved Shadow Word: Pain make early leveling fast and cheap on mana.

Where do you get a wand as a Priest in WoW Forever?

Buy one on the Auction House at level 5, then upgrade to Cookie's Stirring Rod from the Deadmines or a Lesser / Greater Magic Wand made with Enchanting.

Can a Priest heal dungeons without healing talents in WoW Forever?

Yes. At level 20 Ryan healed several Deadmines runs as Shadow using +healing gear, the Staff of Westfall and the Corsair's Overshirt.
