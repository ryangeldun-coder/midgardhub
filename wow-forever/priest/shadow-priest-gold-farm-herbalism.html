Shadow Priest Gold Farm for Herbalists in WoW Forever (Ghost Mushrooms, Level 45+)

Short answer

This is a Shadow Priest gold farm for level 45+ in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever), and the best one if you have Herbalism: Skulk Rock, a cave in The Hinterlands. Ryan Geldun farms it solo on a level 48 Shadow Priest. The cave has up to 3 Ghost Mushrooms spawned at a time, and they sell for about 30 silver each. The sludges inside are easy to kill and drop Scum Covered Bags, silver and grey items. Ryan made about 100 gold here, and it paid for his Alchemy from Skinning to 277 and Transmute: Arcanite. Recorded in November 2026.

Why Skulk Rock Is a Good Priest Gold Farm in WoW Forever

  • Almost nobody farms it. Ryan is alone in the cave nearly all the time; only a few Horde players pass through for a quest.
  • Ghost Mushrooms only grow in caves, so they are scarce, and Warlocks keep Elixir of Shadow Power in demand.
  • The sludges hit softly compared with other mobs at this level, so you can pull everything around you.
  • Every kill drops something you can sell: Scum Covered Bags with coin, and grey items for the vendor.
  • Ryan spent at least a full level here, so it works as an XP farm as well as a gold farm.

How Ryan Farms It As a Shadow Priest

  • DoT up the sludges on the way to each Ghost Mushroom, Psychic Scream when too many are on you, and keep Power Word: Shield up.
  • Pick the mushroom, then move to the next spawn. New mushrooms keep appearing while you clear.
  • He sells every Ghost Mushroom instead of making Elixir of Shadow Power for himself.
  • Consumables he uses: Minor Mana Oil, Greater Intellect Elixir and Lesser Arcane Elixir.
  • Priest vs Warlock: the Warlock has to run and use terrain when mobs reach him. The Priest just shields and keeps casting.

Other Ghost Mushroom Spots

  • Ryan believes Ghost Mushrooms also spawn in a cave in Western Plaguelands with more sludges and elementals.
  • He expects the price to drop a little once more players remember this farm.
