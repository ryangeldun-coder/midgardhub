Discipline Priest Talents at Level 42 (26/0/7)

  • Divine Aegis: non-periodic critical heals create an absorb shield on the target. Ryan says Divine Aegis also works on Penance.
  • Renewed Hope: Flash Heal, Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal and Penance gain critical strike chance on targets with Weakened Soul.
  • Mental Agility 3/3: 10% lower mana cost on Smite, Holy Fire and instant-cast spells such as Shadow Word: Pain and Renew.
  • Holy Precision: more hit chance with Holy spells for questing.
  • Penance, Inner Focus, Meditation and a maxed Improved Power Word: Shield.
  • Wand Specialization 2/2: 25% more wand damage.
  • Spirit Tap 5/5 and Improved Shadow Word: Pain 2/2 in the Shadow tree: pull a pack, Shadow Word: Pain everything, Psychic Scream, wait.

Shadow Priest Talents at Level 42 (2/0/31)

  • Spirit Tap 5/5, Improved Shadow Word: Pain 2/2, Shadow Focus 3/5 (hit chance).
  • Mind Flay plus Improved Mind Flay (3 points total).
  • Improved Mind Blast 5/5 instead of Blackout: Mind Blast cooldown goes from 8 to 6 seconds. Blackout stuns are fun in battlegrounds but add no DPS.
  • Vampiric Embrace, Shadow Weaving 3/3.
  • Devouring Contagion 2/2: Devouring Plague costs 50% less mana, and Devouring Plague spreads to enemies within 10 yards when its target dies.
  • Silence: the spell Ryan missed most on Discipline, for casters that stay at range after a Psychic Scream.
  • Darkness 5/5 (more Shadow damage), chosen over Early Demise (more Shadow Word: Death crit chance on targets at or below 20% health).
  • Shadowform: 10% more Shadow damage in WoW Forever, also on Shadow damage wands (Ryan uses Master Hunter's Wand).
  • Last 2 points: Wand Specialization 2/2, over Dark Inspiration.

Shadow vs Discipline in WoW Forever (Ryan's Take at Level 42)

  • Shadow Priest deals much more burst damage: Mind Flay crits for about 230 at level 42.
  • Discipline Priest has far more survivability: Penance, shields and heals let Ryan solo elite quests such as the elite tiger Bangalash in Stranglethorn Vale.
  • Ryan still enjoys Discipline the most and plans to quest as Shadow for a while.

FAQ

What are the best level 40 Discipline Priest talents in WoW Forever?

Ryan Geldun runs 26/0/7 at level 42: Divine Aegis, Renewed Hope, Mental Agility, Penance, Inner Focus, Meditation, Improved Power Word: Shield and Wand Specialization, plus Spirit Tap.

What is a good Shadow Priest build at level 40 in WoW Forever?

Ryan's 2/0/31 build: Spirit Tap, Improved Mind Blast, Mind Flay, Shadow Weaving, Devouring Contagion, Silence, Darkness and Shadowform.

Does Shadowform increase wand damage in WoW Forever?

Yes, according to Ryan: the 10% Shadow damage bonus from Shadowform also applies to wands that deal Shadow damage.

Is Discipline or Shadow better for leveling a Priest in WoW Forever?

Shadow deals more damage. Discipline survives better and can solo elite quests. Ryan prefers Discipline but uses dual spec to quest as Shadow.
