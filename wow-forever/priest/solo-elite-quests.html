Can a Priest Solo Elite Quests in WoW Forever?

Ryan Geldun November 18, 2026 3:17 video
Short answer

Can a Priest solo elite quests in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? Yes. In this video Ryan Geldun solos Mor'Ladim (Morladim), the level 35 elite skeleton at Raven Hill Cemetery in Duskwood, on a level 35 Priest with no group. The method: kite the elite around fences it cannot jump over, keep Shadow Word: Pain up, wand it down and heal with Renew. Recorded in November 2026.

Key takeaways

  • A Priest can solo most elite quests in WoW Forever at the quest's level by using terrain.
  • Elite mobs in WoW Forever cannot jump over fences, but players can. Jumping over a fence makes the elite walk around it while your damage keeps ticking.
  • The Priest solo loop for elites: apply Shadow Word: Pain, kite, wand, and keep Renew on yourself.
  • Mor'Ladim is a melee-only elite that hits very hard, so he deals no damage as long as he can't reach you.
  • Against caster elites (for example ones that cast Shadow Bolt), apply your DoT and then hide behind a building to break line of sight.
  • Buildings break line of sight in WoW Forever. Fences do not, and some trees and hills do not either.
  • Shadow Priests can slow an elite with Mind Flay, but Mind Flay is channeled and keeps you standing still, so terrain kiting is usually safer.

FAQ

Can a Priest solo elite quests in WoW Forever?

Yes. A Priest can solo most elite quests at the quest's level by kiting the elite around fences, keeping Shadow Word: Pain up, wanding and healing with Renew.

How do you solo Mor'Ladim in Duskwood?

Kite Mor'Ladim around the fences of Raven Hill Cemetery. He is melee only and cannot jump fences, so he can't hit you while you wand and DoT him.

How do you solo caster elites as a Priest?

Apply your DoT, then break line of sight behind a building when the elite casts. Fences and some trees do not block line of sight.
