How to Heal Dungeons as a Priest in WoW Forever

Ryan Geldun November 15, 2026 10:08 video
Short answer

How do you heal dungeons as a Priest in WoW Forever? Keep the tank alive with Power Word: Shield, cast one Heal only when it's needed, and spend the rest of your time doing damage. Wand, dot enemies with Shadow Word: Pain and land last hits to proc Spirit Tap so you almost never run out of mana. Recorded live in a WoW Forever dungeon at level 30+, November 2026. Most groups in the Looking for Group tool are short on healers, so this is the fastest way to get into dungeons.

Key takeaways

  • Your main job is keeping the tank alive. Pre-shield the tank and heal only when health actually drops. Topping up a tank with high health wastes mana.
  • Don't stack Renew on a shielded tank at full health. It ticks for nothing. Use Renew when the shield is down.
  • Healers have no fixed rotation. React to the situation. The safest state is a shielded, topped-up tank while you wand.
  • You don't need Discipline. Shadow Priests can heal dungeons with the same toolkit, minus Penance.
  • Spirit Tap is your mana engine. Land last hits with Mind Blast, Penance or Shadow Word: Death. With the Meditation talent (Discipline tree) you don't have to chase every proc.
  • DPS while healing: in my group I did around 50 DPS while healing, next to a Shaman doing about 60.
  • If the tank is under-geared or taking crits, stop DPSing and focus on heals.
  • Any hybrid can try healing: buy cheap intellect and healing-power gear from the Auction House. Healers shouldn't take damage, so cloth is fine even on a Paladin, Shaman or Druid.
  • I got almost all my gear from dungeons, always queued as a healer, even as Shadow.

FAQ

Can a Shadow Priest heal dungeons in WoW Forever?

Yes. Shadow keeps Power Word: Shield, Renew and Heal. You only lose Penance.

How do Priests manage mana while healing?

Proc Spirit Tap with last hits (Shadow Word: Death, Mind Blast, Penance) and avoid overhealing tanks with high health.

Is healing harder than DPS in dungeons?

Often not. DPS players also have to handle crowd control and keep their damage up. A Priest healer can still add a lot of damage.
