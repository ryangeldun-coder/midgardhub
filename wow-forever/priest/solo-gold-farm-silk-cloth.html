Can a Priest Solo Farm Gold in WoW Forever? (Silk Cloth Spot)

Ryan Geldun November 17, 2026 3:51 video
Short answer

Can a Priest solo farm gold in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? Yes. In this video Ryan Geldun farms Silk Cloth and Wool Cloth solo on a level 34 Discipline Priest in the Dawning Wood Catacombs, the crypt under Raven Hill Cemetery in Duskwood. The zombies there are level 26-29, respawn fast and drop only stackable loot, so you can farm for a whole level. Recorded in November 2026.

Key takeaways

  • The Dawning Wood Catacombs in Duskwood are a good Silk Cloth and Wool Cloth farm for level 30-35 characters in WoW Forever.
  • Zombies in the Dawning Wood Catacombs are level 26-29, so a level 34 Priest kills them easily and they often miss.
  • Silk Cloth and Wool Cloth each sold for about 1 silver on the WoW Forever Auction House in November 2026. Prices change.
  • Everything the catacomb zombies drop is stackable (cloth, coins, Tomb Dust), so your bags don't fill up.
  • The zombies respawn fast. Other players only pass through for a quest chain and leave, so competition is low.
  • Plague Spreaders cast Plague Mind, a disease that drains about 21 mana every 10 seconds and gets recast. Priests can dispel it.
  • No herbs spawn inside the Dawning Wood Catacombs. Herbalists can clear the crypt, then pick Grave Moss, Kingsblood and Bruiseweed in Raven Hill Cemetery outside while the zombies respawn.
  • Multi-pulling with Power Word: Shield, DoTs and Mind Blast makes the cloth add up faster.
  • Other classes with self-healing or cleave, especially Druids with Swipe, can farm the Dawning Wood Catacombs too.

FAQ

Where can you farm Silk Cloth at level 30 in WoW Forever?

In the Dawning Wood Catacombs under Raven Hill Cemetery in Duskwood. The level 26-29 zombies drop Silk Cloth and Wool Cloth and respawn quickly.

Can a Priest solo farm in WoW Forever?

Yes. Power Word: Shield, DoTs and disease dispels let a Priest solo farm safely, even against mobs that drain mana.

How much is Silk Cloth worth in WoW Forever?

About 1 silver per Silk Cloth on the Auction House in November 2026. Prices change over time.

Can other classes farm the Dawning Wood Catacombs?

Yes. Any class with self-healing or AoE/cleave does well there. Druids are a strong fit.
