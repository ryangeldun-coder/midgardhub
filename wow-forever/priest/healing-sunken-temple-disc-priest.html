Disc Priest Notes From This Run

  • Penance rank 3 comes at level 50 and heals a lot.
  • Power Word: Shield on the tank before every pull. On easy trash one shield lasts the whole pack.
  • When the healing is calm, Ryan adds damage with Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast and his wand, and keeps about half his mana for emergencies.
  • Spirit Tap still works for a Discipline Priest in WoW Forever, according to Ryan.
  • Shadow Word: Death hits much softer in Disc than in Shadow spec, because there is no Shadowform, so it is harder to time for Spirit Tap.
  • The trolls hex the tank and turn players into frogs. A small Gnome tank is hard to watch without addons.
  • Use Fade when a shield or heal pulls aggro.
  • Buff Shadow Protection before the Jammal'an and Eranikus fights.

Bosses

  • Atal'alarion (optional, level 50): summoned from the snake statues, knocks players up.
  • Hakkar event: kill the adds, burn their blood in the braziers, and Avatar of Hakkar (level 53) appears.
  • Jammal'an the Prophet: mind controls a player. Ryan could not dispel it on the tank.
  • The dragons: Dreamscythe, Weaver, Hazzas and Morphaz.
  • Shade of Eranikus: level 55, about 46,000 health, immune to nature damage.

Loot for a Priest

  • Kilt of the Atal'ai Prophet: Stamina, Intellect and Spirit. Ryan needed on it.
  • Blood of Morphaz: starts the priest quest that takes you to Felwood.
  • Essence of Eranikus: turned in at the Essence Font for a trinket.
  • Spire of Hakkar (staff) went to the mage.

FAQ

Can a Discipline Priest heal Sunken Temple in WoW Forever?

Yes. Ryan healed the full run at level 50 with no addons, and it went easier than he expected, mostly thanks to a strong group.

Does Spirit Tap work for Discipline Priest in WoW Forever?

Yes, according to Ryan. He triggers it with Shadow Word: Death and wand kills.

What level is Shade of Eranikus in WoW Forever?

Level 55, with about 46,000 health. He is immune to nature damage.

Can you dispel Jammal'an's mind control?

Ryan could not dispel it on his tank in this run.

How to Heal Dungeons as a Priest in WoW Forever

How do you heal dungeons as a Priest in WoW Forever? Keep the tank alive with Power Word: Shield, cast one Heal only when it's needed, and spend the rest of your time doing damage. Wand, dot enemies with Shadow Word: Pain and land last hits to proc Spirit Tap so you almost never run out of mana. Recorded live in a WoW Forever dungeon at level 30+, November 2026.
