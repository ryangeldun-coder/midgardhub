How a Disc Priest Makes Mount Gold in WoW Forever

  • Before level 30, a priest in WoW Forever earns more by leveling professions than by farming, because level 20–30 loot is mostly low value apart from Wool Cloth.
  • At levels 28–30, the ghouls in the Raven Hill Cemetery catacombs (Duskwood) drop Wool Cloth, Silk Cloth and vendor items.
  • The turtles along the Lordamere Lake shore in Alterac Mountains (about level 30–31) are easy to skin and give leather, Turtle Meat and Speckled Shell Fragments. The shore also has Liferoot and the occasional Goldthorn for Herbalism.
  • Mountain Lions and Hulking Mountain Lions in Alterac Mountains die quickly to Shadow Word: Pain, can be skinned and drop Bristly Whiskers, which vendor for about 7 silver each.
  • The Syndicate humanoids around Strahnbrad in Alterac Mountains drop Silk Cloth, Raw Silver and Raw Copper. The nearby ogres drop similar loot and respawn quickly.
  • The yeti cave in Hillsbrad Foothills is very good for skinners, but only when no one else is questing there.
  • Scarlet Monastery gave Ryan the bulk of his mount gold. A healer gets invited almost instantly (especially for Armory), has little loot competition from cloth classes, and warrior or paladin groups clear it very fast.
  • While waiting for a Scarlet Monastery group, Ryan solo-killed the first mobs in the Graveyard wing. They drop about 5–7 silver each.
  • Don't buy every spell rank before level 40. Skipping some ranks saves gold for the riding skill.
  • Skinning and Herbalism are quick to catch up on: about 30 minutes in Elwynn Forest gets both to 75, then Westfall and northwest Duskwood.

Can a healer farm gold in WoW Forever?
