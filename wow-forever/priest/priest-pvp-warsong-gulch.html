Can a Priest PvP in WoW Forever? (Warsong Gulch)

Ryan Geldun November 11, 2026 17:04 video
Short answer

Can a Priest PvP in World of Warcraft: Forever (WoW Forever)? Yes. In this video Ryan Geldun takes a level 26 Shadow Priest into Warsong Gulch (20-29 bracket) during Warsong Weekend, unscripted and not playing Holy, and wins with 4 killing blows, almost 10,000 damage and almost 10,000 healing. Recorded in November 2026.

Key takeaways

  • A Shadow Priest is very strong in early WoW Forever PvP: DoTs on as many enemies as possible, Mind Flay to slow runners, Psychic Scream when they collapse on you, and Power Word: Shield on teammates while mana lasts.
  • The Priest PvP playstyle is the same as PvE leveling: dot, fear, Mind Flay, wand.
  • 2 points in Shadow Focus are worth it for PvP, because a resisted Shadow Word: Pain hurts.
  • Hybrid gear with both healing power and spell damage (Shadowfang Keep drops, Pristine Circlet) lets a Shadow Priest deal damage and heal in the same battleground.
  • In WoW Forever, DoTs and Mind Flay can stun (shown in the video).
  • In this match a Druid and a Rogue on the enemy team were the hardest opponents, including getting sapped by the Rogue.
  • Shields count as healing on the scoreboard, which is how a Shadow Priest reached almost 10,000 healing.
  • Dungeon healing tip from the end of the video: Renew vs Lesser Heal.

FAQ

Is Shadow Priest good in PvP in WoW Forever?

Yes. At level 26 in Warsong Gulch, a Shadow Priest can spread DoTs, slow, fear and shield teammates. Ryan's match ended in a win with 4 killing blows and about 10,000 damage and 10,000 healing.

What talents should a Priest use for low-level PvP in WoW Forever?

Spirit Tap, Improved Shadow Word: Pain and Mind Flay, plus 2 points in Shadow Focus for hit.

What gear should a Priest wear for PvP while leveling?

Hybrid healing and spell damage gear, for example Shadowfang Keep drops and a crafted Pristine Circlet.
