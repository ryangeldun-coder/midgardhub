Key takeaways

  • You can heal dungeons in WoW Forever without any healing addons. Ryan Geldun has played healers since Mists of Pandaria and never used healing addons.
  • Group your keybinds into clusters: damage spells together, healing spells together, panic buttons right next to W, A, S, D.
  • Penance has a 12 second cooldown and a Disc Priest casts it almost on cooldown, so it belongs on a key next to the movement keys (R).
  • Renew and Psychic Scream work as panic buttons while running away, so both sit next to the movement keys (Q and E).
  • Targeting keybinds (target self, target party member 1-4) can be set in Options, Keybindings, by searching "target".
  • Holding Shift and scrolling the mouse wheel switches action bars, which is handy for buffing between pulls.
  • Raid-style party frames: Edit Mode, Party Frames, tick "Use Raid-Style Party Frames". They show far more than the default party frames.
  • Display Class Colors colors party frame names by class (Paladin pink, Shaman blue, Priest white, Warlock purple, Rogue yellow), so you can see at a glance who is taking damage.
  • Show Numbers for Cooldowns adds a countdown number on every spell on cooldown, with no addon needed.
  • Spells you never need under pressure, like Inner Fire, can stay unbound and be clicked.

FAQ

Do you need addons to heal in WoW Forever?

No. The built-in raid-style party frames, class colors and cooldown numbers cover the basics. Ryan Geldun heals WoW Forever dungeons on a Disc Priest with no healing addons.

What are good Disc Priest keybinds in WoW Forever?

Put Penance, Lesser Heal, Renew, Flash Heal and Psychic Scream on keys next to W, A, S, D (R, F, Q, T, E), damage spells on 1-4 and Power Word: Shield on F1.

How do you turn on raid-style party frames in WoW Forever?

Open Edit Mode, select Party Frames and tick "Use Raid-Style Party Frames". Then turn on Display Class Colors in Options.

How do you show cooldown numbers without an addon in WoW Forever?

Open Options, search "cooldowns" and turn on "Show Numbers for Cooldowns".
