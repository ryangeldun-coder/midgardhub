Keep Midgard Community Hub going

The database, the calculators, the guides and the Kafra bot are built and maintained by one person, given away for free, with only light ads and no paywalls. If it's saved you some time, here are the ways to give something back - most of them don't cost a thing.

Free ways that genuinely help

If money is off the table, no problem - these matter just as much for a project this size.

Share it with your guild

Drop a link in your guild chat, use Midgard Hub instead of the sites that copied it, and link it when someone asks where the data is from. Reach is the thing that keeps a niche project alive.

Add Kafra to your Discord

Kafra puts the whole database in your server with /kafra. Every reply links back here - it's free, one click, and it spreads the site.

Join the community Discord

An active community is what makes the data stay current. Come hang out, run dungeons, and help other players find their way around.

Subscribe on YouTube

The site grew out of the Ryan Geldun channel and still runs on it. Subscribing is free and it directly helps the whole project.

Report data errors

Spotted a wrong drop rate or a missing item? Tell us on Discord or via contact. Corrections keep the database trustworthy for everyone.

Keep ads unblocked here

Ads on the site are light, consent-based, and the main way it pays for itself. Allowlisting midgardhub.com costs you nothing and it's the quietest way to help.

Where the support goes

Midgard Community Hub runs on a domain, a handful of API and hosting costs, and a lot of unpaid hours keeping the data in step with the game. It's one person, no team.

Support doesn't buy anything - there's no members-only data, no ad-free tier, no locked tools, and there never will be. It just keeps the site online and the database current. Everything here stays free whether you give or not.
